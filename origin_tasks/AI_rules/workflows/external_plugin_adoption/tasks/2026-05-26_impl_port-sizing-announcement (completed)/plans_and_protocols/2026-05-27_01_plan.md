## Han Sizing Mechanic (re-authored in our voice)

### Bands (file-count semantics for code-* skills)

| Band | Files in scope | Layers touched | Cross-cutting concerns |
|------|---------------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Small** | ≤3 | 1 | None |
| **Medium** | 4–10 | ≤2 | ≤1 (new dep, schema change) |
| **Large** | >10 | 3 | Multiple, or any security/PII/auth surface |

**Default**: Small. Escalate only on a concrete signal from the table. Security/PII/auth always escalates to Large.

**Override**: `size_override: small|medium|large` in goal.md YAML, or explicit "size: X" in skill invocation.

### Roster calibration for code-complex

| Band | Architecture-advisor depth | Max impl batches | Test-fix rounds |
|------|--------------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Small | Quick plan | 2 batches | 1 per test file |
| Medium | Standard plan | 4 batches | 2 per test file |
| Large | Thorough plan (trade-offs + rejected-alternatives section required) | No cap | As needed |

### Screen-count bands for ui-create-scribble

Derivation: `sum = count(ACs in requirements.md) + count(flow steps in steps[]) + count(exception paths in those ACs)`

| Sum | Band | Expected screens |
|-----|------|-----------------|
| ≤5 | **Small** | 1–3 screens |
| 6–12 | **Medium** | 4–7 screens |
| >12 | **Large** | 8+ screens |

Phase-depth calibration:

| Band | Phase 2 auto-review rounds | 3rd validation pass |
|------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| Small | 1 round (v1 → v2 only) | No |
| Medium | 1 round per odd version (current behavior) | No |
| Large | 1 round per odd version + completeness re-check | Yes — spawn completeness agent before Phase 5 |
