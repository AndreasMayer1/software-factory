# Opus Plan: Update Self-User Personas for Self-Management Focus

## Objective

Revise the 4 self-user personas (David, Lisa, Hanna, Michael) to sharpen their distinction from client personas by emphasizing the **"self-management / intrinsic motivation"** context versus the client personas' **"therapy / compliance"** context.

**Core distinction**:
- **Clients**: "Help me fulfill my therapist's plan" (external drive/obligation)
- **Self-users**: "Help me understand/regulate myself because no expert is available" (internal drive/necessity)

## Analysis Summary

### Current State Assessment

All 4 personas already exist and have good foundational content. However, they need refinement to:

1. **Sharpen the "no therapist" context** - These users are alone, the app is their only anchor
2. **Emphasize self-management struggles** - They must build their own systems without guidance
3. **Clarify distinct pain points** - Different from clients who have external structure

### Key Contrasts to Emphasize

| Self-User | Client Counterpart | Key Distinction |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| David (ADHD Self-Manager) | Sophie (ADHD Client) | Sophie gets structure from Dr. Sarah; David must build his own and fails repeatedly |
| Lisa (Waitlist Bridger) | All clients | Lisa is in "The Gap" - diagnosed but no access to care (6-month wait) |
| Hanna (Sleepless) | No direct counterpart | Unique nighttime-only usage context (2-4 AM, in bed) |
| Michael (High-Performer) | No direct counterpart | Denies illness, refuses therapy, privacy-paranoid, functional burnout |

### Specific Modifications Needed

#### David (PERSONA-008)
- Add explicit "Shiny Object Syndrome" - 20 apps installed, deleted after 3 days
- Clarify: Problem is not **starting** (like Max) but **maintaining** - novelty death
- Emphasize: Needs instant reward + ultra-low friction (Quick Capture)
- Add contrast to Sophie: David has no one giving him "the paper" to follow

#### Lisa (PERSONA-005)
- Strengthen "The Gap" context - 6-month wait, diagnosed but unserved
- Add: Feeling of abandonment ("Am I sick enough to deserve help?")
- Emphasize: Collecting "evidence" for first session to be taken seriously
- Add: Uncertainty about what's relevant (needs psychoeducation, no therapist to explain)

#### Hanna (PERSONA-007)
- Strengthen: "Gedankenkarussell" as the enemy she fights alone
- Emphasize: "Brain dump" need - externalize thoughts to enable sleep
- Clarify: Light is absolute dealbreaker (partner, circadian, OLED)
- Add: Morning frustration with illegible nighttime notes
- Note: Implicit senior cluster (sleep disorders common in elderly)

#### Michael (PERSONA-006)
- Strengthen: Therapy refusal - "I'm not crazy, just stressed"
- Emphasize: Wearable user missing the psyche link ("why did I sleep badly?")
- Clarify: Extreme privacy fears - app can't look like "health app" at work
- Add: Views emotions as "data points for optimization"
- Strengthen: Isolation - no outlet (not boss, not partner, not friends)
