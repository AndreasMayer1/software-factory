   - **Nocturnal rumination** (brain_dump) is a hallmark of depression, not ADHD. ADHD causes racing thoughts, but the *lying awake ruminating about tomorrow's meeting* pattern is classic depression/anxiety.
   - **Forgetting objects** (forgotten_protocol) occurs in depression too — memory fog, executive dysfunction from low drive, and cognitive impairment are well-documented depression symptoms.

2. **The "ADHD traits" in the scenarios are actually mixed.** Looking at the specific text:
   - "Wall of Awful" (brain_dump line 142) — this IS an ADHD-specific term. But the barrier described (can't get up from bed) is equally a depression drive-disturbance symptom.

   - The persona reference says "Depression/ADHD" in brain_dump (line 42) but Max's actual persona says `conditions: ["Major Depression", "Burnout"]` — no ADHD.

Moving Max's failure version to Sophie would give her two scenarios in the same category, while leaving Max with zero transfer scenarios.

4. **The scenarios fit Max's depression narrative.** Max's persona describes: "White Sheet Syndrome," physical inertia, memory fog, "Parking Lot Syndrome." Both scenarios demonstrate these depression-specific pain points. The scenarios just need their ADHD-specific language cleaned up.
