# Task Completion Skills

## Overview

Requirements for the `task-complete` and `task-complete-bugfix` skills: the mechanisms that close tasks, verify quality gates, update requirement coverage, and commit changes.

## Purpose

The `task-complete` and `task-complete-bugfix` skills exist and are documented in `.claude/skills/task-complete/SKILL.md` and `.claude/skills/task-complete-bugfix/SKILL.md`. They were authored before this epic existed. This placeholder marks the location where a formal requirement will be written.

## Deferred (YAGNI)

### Full requirement authoring
**Why deferred:** Both skills are stable and actively enforced (they are mandatory last steps in every task). A requirement is most valuable when a change is planned.
**Reopen when:** A task is planned that modifies the quality-gate execution, coverage update logic, or commit behavior of either skill.
