### BLOCKER 1 — `ui-scribble-heuristics-reviewer` has no grounding corpus
The current `ui-create-scribble` Phase 2 does NOT do Nielsen/Saffer/dark-pattern heuristics
review. That capability is **new** — introduced by the split per amendment A-2. But no
heuristics corpus exists anywhere in `doc/presentation/` (grep found only an unrelated
"heuristic" mention in a linter doc). Options:
- **1a (recommended):** Author a `doc/presentation/heuristics/` corpus first (via
  `doc-update-guidelines`), then the agent applies it. Keeps "don't invent behavior" intact.
  This is arguably its own task / requirement.
- **1b:** Bake a compact heuristics checklist directly into the agent prompt (faster, but
  embeds un-reviewed UX doctrine into a factory agent — weaker grounding).
- **1c:** Ship the agent as a thin scaffold that reads whatever exists in `doc/presentation/`
  and explicitly defers the heuristics corpus to a follow-up (agent is a no-op until corpus
  exists). Lowest risk, partial value.
