- **Key Decision** + **Common Pitfall** added (deterministic + diff-bounded
  human review is the control; LLM-as-gate rejected with reasons).

## Rationale (LLM-as-gate rejected)
Circular trust (checker/launcher itself unreviewed, runs first); LLM not a sound
boundary vs adversarial code; prompt-injectable from files under review;
non-deterministic; false assurance erodes review. Sound controls: diff-bounded
human review + deterministic denylist + advisory-only LLM + Sandbox containment.
